Derivative WebP files are resized copies of those photographs (no other modification). Attribution,
licence and source link are shown in the application's image credits screen and in each destination
record's `imageCredit` field.

**CC BY-SA note:** resized/cropped derivatives of CC BY-SA images are distributed under the same
licence. See `docs/LICENSING.md`.

Photographs whose licensing could not be verified (e.g. the Mirik bridge image) are stored but
`displayed: false`. Places without a verified licensed photograph are shown with a designed
"Photograph unavailable" state, never with an unrelated or AI-generated stand-in.
